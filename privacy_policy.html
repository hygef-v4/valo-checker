Privacy Policy for ValoCheck

Last updated: August 05, 2026

ValoCheck ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the ValoCheck mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

ValoCheck does not collect, store, or sell any personal identifying information on external servers managed by us.

To provide daily store rotations and match statistics, ValoCheck uses official Riot Games authentication via a secure in-app WebView directly communicating with Riot Games servers. Your login credentials (username and password) are entered directly into Riot Games' official authentication service and are never stored or transmitted to ValoCheck servers.

2. Local Data Storage

Authentication tokens and local preferences (such as wishlist items or saved settings) are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage. You can clear this data at any time by logging out or uninstalling the application.

3. Third-Party Services

The app communicates directly with official Riot Games APIs to fetch user stats and store data. We encourage you to review Riot Games' Privacy Policy regarding how they handle data.

4. Children's Privacy

ValoCheck is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Disclaimer

ValoCheck is an unofficial fan companion app and is not endorsed by, directly affiliated with, maintained, authorized, or sponsored by Riot Games, Inc.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our project support repository or support email.
